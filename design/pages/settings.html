About

BLACKOUT
BLACKOUT is a lightweight Android utility that allows users to hide the display while keeping the current application active.

Designed with a privacy-first, minimal, and battery-conscious approach.

Privacy

Your Data Stays On Your Device
BLACKOUT does not upload your usage data.

Statistics, sessions, and preferences remain stored locally unless future cloud synchronization is explicitly enabled by you.
